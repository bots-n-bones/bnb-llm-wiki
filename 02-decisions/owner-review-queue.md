## Review protocol

For each project, the owner can answer with one of these outcomes:

- **Approve as canon:** the page is accurate and current.
- **Keep as draft:** useful evidence, but not an approved statement.
- **Archive / supersede:** the content is obsolete or should not appear in normal search.
- **Correct:** provide the specific replacement fact; the source remains preserved.

The owner explicitly approved the full grouped review on 2026-08-21. Each page
retains its source record, status, and citation trail; restricted and archive-only
materials remain outside this promotion.

## Not part of this queue

Media, archives, macro-capable Office files, restricted material, and live
XLSX values remain outside canonical search. They can be located by provenance
without being interpreted as documentation.

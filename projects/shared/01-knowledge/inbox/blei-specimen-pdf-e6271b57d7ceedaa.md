 Stretching and squishing fonts 
can distort the relative thickness of 
horizontal and vertical stems. While this 
practice is often criticized, Blei views 
these effects as acceptable from a 
creative perspective.
 However, elements like shoulders, 
bowls, and terminals can suffer, leading 
to a design that may appear amateurish 
or rushed, along with potential issues 
in letter spacing. Blei addresses these 
concerns with careful corrections, 
resulting in a more polished typeface.
 It’s a partially geometric, sans-
serif, uniwidth typeface available in three 
weights and five widths, spanning three 
distinct styles: Straight, Inflated and 
Rounded.
 With a character set of 850+ glyphs 
and some OpenType features such as 
Stylistic Set 1 (White Button), Blei allows 
for versatile design options.
 You can highlight all characters 
by framing them, with several choices 
for starting and ending the frames 
using different brackets. This flexibility 
ensures clear and legible content, 
making Blei ideal for applications with 
both limited and generous space.

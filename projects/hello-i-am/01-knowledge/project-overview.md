# Hello I Am project overview

## Purpose and scope

HELLOIAM.AM is presented as a cultural introduction system: Armenia is introduced through objects, food, rituals, streets, sounds, and small details rather than as a destination checklist.

## Brand and editorial structure

- `HELLOIAM.AM` is the brand shell; “Meet Armenia” is the positioning layer.
- `HELLO, I AM AM` is the launch phrase and `I AM _` is the repeatable editorial engine.
- Documented territories include food, culture, streets, objects, sounds, people, places, seasons, and future.
- The launch sequence is identity → category maps → deep dives.

## Audience and guardrails

The stated launch audience is English-speaking, visually literate, and culturally curious. The intended tone is quiet, tactile, editorial, culturally specific, and warm without sentimentality. Tourism-board language, listicles, stock-travel aesthetics, glossy food advertising, and AI over-rendering are explicitly rejected.

# Hello I Am cultural-media case overview

> Draft synthesis. It describes the source's intended launch system and is not
> evidence that every workflow or asset is current in production.

The source frames Hello I Am as a cultural introduction to Armenia through
food, streets, objects, sounds, and other details rather than a conventional
tourism guide. It proposes one linked brand and editorial system:

- **HELLOIAM.AM** as the brand and domain;
- **Meet Armenia** for positioning and partnerships;
- **HELLO, I AM AM** as a launch phrase; and
- **I AM _** as the scalable editorial pattern for topics such as food,
  culture, streets, objects, and sounds.

The proposed production flow is `topic → brief → copy → cover → generate →
QA → publish`. The source assigns briefs, rubrics, weekly rhythm, and launch
tracking to the structural layer; prompt assembly and generation tools to the
automation layer; and naming, lockup, palette, typography, and templates to
the brand layer.

For launch, the source proposes a weekly cadence of three feed posts, two
story packs, and one reel, with a 36-post map that begins from identity and
category maps before deeper subject posts. These are source claims requiring
owner confirmation, not live publishing commitments.

# Hello I Am content rubric map

## Two-layer model

The source distinguishes editorial territory from delivery format. Territories answer what cultural world is explored; rubrics define a post’s editorial role; formats define delivery as carousel, reel-first, or hybrid.

## Documented core rubrics

The rubric set includes First Hello, Category Map, Deep Dive, Objects of Armenia, Street Feelings, Rituals and Habits, Sounds of Armenia, People/Archetypes, relatable “not weird” observations, and seasonal signals.

## Format guidance

Examples of visual delivery include standard carousel, comic panels, hero image with captions, grid map, infographic list, and photo essay. Objects are used as cultural keys; deep dives combine fact, atmosphere, and recognition; stories may test audience preferences.

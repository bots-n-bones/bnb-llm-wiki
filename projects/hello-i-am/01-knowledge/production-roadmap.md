# Hello I Am launch production roadmap

## Scope

The work plan covers the Instagram start: editorial canon, first-month structure, post and story formats, copy briefs, storyboards, and video handoff manifests. It explicitly excludes full website execution, long-form editorial, partnerships, and the travel-product layer.

## Phases

1. System setup — marked done in the source.
2. Launch copy — marked next; briefs and copy for the first twelve posts.
3. Story layer — two packs per week tied to feed themes.
4. Storyboards — after text approval.
5. Video handoff — manifests, assets, timing, and production notes after storyboard approval.

## Roles and controls

Writer/strategist, designer, editor/video operator, and coordinator have distinct documented responsibilities. Work without a rubric or a place in the monthly arc should be blocked. The immediate source-listed next action is to prepare the three identity briefs and two Week 1 story packs.

# Hello I Am Instagram launch system

## Channel roles

- Feed establishes identity, recurring visual anchors, and deep cultural entries.
- Stories provide proximity, interaction, and audience signals between feed posts.
- Reels extend the strongest feed ideas into motion and atmosphere.

## Launch cadence and arc

The documented baseline is three feed posts, two story packs, and one reel adaptation per week. Month one progresses from identity, through category maps, to deep dives.

## Production flow

The source describes a common sequence: topic → brief → copy → cover direction → asset list → storyboard → reel adaptation → story support → export. A completed feed item includes its thesis, slide structure, final copy, cover direction, prompts, assets, and storyboard notes.

## Validation

A new topic should be placeable in a rubric, weekly slot, post brief, storyboard, and reel. If it cannot, the source recommends simplifying the launch system.

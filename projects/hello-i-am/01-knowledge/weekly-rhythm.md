# Hello I Am weekly publishing rhythm

## Weekly slots

The documented weekly sequence is Meaning, Character, and Atmosphere, supported by a Closer Look story pack and an Interaction story pack. The suggested cadence places these across the week while keeping the sequence recognizable.

## Four-week launch

1. Identity: three `I AM _` introduction posts.
2. Category maps I: food, culture, and streets.
3. Category maps II plus the first deep dive: objects, sounds, and lavash.
4. Deep dives: matsun, dolma, and wine.

## Monthly balance

The source targets roughly 35–40% food and rituals, 25–30% streets and atmosphere, 15–20% objects, 10–15% identity/meta/humor, and 5–10% tests and audience-response formats.

# Content OS tone and voice

## Calibration

The documented voice is low in aggression, low-to-medium in warmth, high in confidence and directness, and avoids irony. Key lines are normally short, with one idea per paragraph and bullets for lists.

## Platform differences

- LinkedIn: precise, structured, minimal emoji, ending in a takeaway.
- Telegram: deeper lab notes, diagrams, failure analysis, and substantive build-in-public context.
- Instagram: minimal and declarative, with visuals carrying most of the message.

## Guardrails

The voice avoids guaranteed-result claims, agency language, hype, generic AI-magic framing, and outputs without system context. Work in progress should be labeled as such.

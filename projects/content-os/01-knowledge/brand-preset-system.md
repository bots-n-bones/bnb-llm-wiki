# Content OS brand preset system

## Required inputs

The guide expects positioning/tone materials, site or landing copy, real case examples, visual guidance, and optionally strategy or research. It instructs authors not to invent facts when evidence is incomplete.

## Preset package

A complete brand preset contains twelve numbered documents plus a README. The numbered set covers identity, tone, messaging, vocabulary, anti-positioning, proof, conversion, emotional calibration, visual alignment, positive and negative examples, and a master configuration.

## Quality gates

Each document carries source attribution. The completion checklist checks identity primitives, a messaging checklist, case evidence or explicit stubs, hierarchy links, shared-layer references, and a concise brand summary. The source also protects existing non-preset files from unapproved deletion or rename.

# SVMPX Electronics Q4 2025 Roadmap

[Open original on Google Drive](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1EA9B6HfFkgAsHyZc5o4XpOR8giXM7wPPw04AJaoHuUA/edit)

## Confirmed content

The source is explicitly dated Q4 2025. It describes eight weighted blocks:
client comments, pipeline finance, target-price fitting, Client Needs,
personalized interfaces, Clothing migration, the Brutto bot, and Logistics
chains 2.0. It asks the client for five groups of process and requirements data.

It records a direction in which the Brutto bot handles notifications,
statistics, and reports while primary work moves to Airtable interfaces.

## Evidence limitation

The source does not state current completion, current ownership, approved dates,
or whether its priorities and bot direction remain valid. Logistics chains 2.0
is labeled `+20%`, so the listed weights cannot safely be normalized as a
capacity allocation.
